Click a day and type its area letter, or pick a color and click days.

Typing on a day

A B C M D X 9 L OA, B, C, ME, DMG, Xpress, 999, Learning Center, Overgoods. Moves to the next day.
Shift+a letterAdds a second area. For 999/B type 9 then Shift+B.
-Day off (a regular day off in Regular schedule).
Backspace or DeleteClears the day. On one day of vacation you set by hand, it removes the vacation. With several days selected, or with the Clear brush, vacation you set by hand is skipped.
VMarks the day, or every selected day, as vacation (VAC). Press it again to remove. Specific dates only.
HHides the asked-off request on the day, or on every selected day. Press it again to show it. Specific dates only.
Enter or right-clickOn a day with an asked-off request or hand-set vacation, opens a small menu. The menu key does the same.
Arrow keysMove to another day.
Enter or SpacePuts the picked color on the day.

Several days at once

Shift+arrow keysSelects a block of days from the one you are on.
Shift+clickSelects a block of days (with no color picked).
Ctrl+clickAdds or removes one day.
Type with days selectedA letter, - or Backspace fills every selected day. Vacation you set by hand is skipped; press V to remove it.
EscBack to one day. Again to drop the picked color.

Copy, paste and the rest

Ctrl+CCopies the selected days, also as text for Excel.
Ctrl+VPastes. One copied day fills every selected day; a block repeats to fill them. Time off is never changed.
Ctrl+ZUndoes the last change. A paste or a fill is one step.
Ctrl+PPrints the grid as Screenshot view shows it.
Click a headingAdmin sorts by name; a day groups by area. Click again for My order.
